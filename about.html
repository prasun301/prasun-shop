About Prasun Shop

Practical guides and technical documentation for engineers and professionals.

Welcome to Prasun Shop, an independent digital publishing platform founded and authored by Prasun Barua. Our mission is to break down complex engineering concepts into clear, actionable, and intuitive guides.

Whether you are looking to understand residential solar systems, dive deep into electrical engineering fundamentals, or explore the intricacies of semiconductor manufacturing, these publications are designed to help you make informed technical decisions.

All books are delivered as high-quality digital PDFs instantly upon purchase, ensuring you get immediate access to the resources you need.
