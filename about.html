About PRASUN SHOP

Practical technology, thoughtfully selected.

PRASUN SHOP is an independent online store focused on practical technology products and useful digital resources. Our goal is simple: make useful products easier to discover, understand, and purchase through a clean and straightforward shopping experience.

What is PRASUN SHOP?

PRASUN SHOP brings together a curated selection of technology and everyday products across categories such as smart home devices, networking equipment, electronics accessories, power products, lighting, cameras, tools, and other useful technology solutions.

We aim to keep the store focused rather than overwhelming. Products are selected with practical usefulness, clear presentation, and everyday applications in mind.

Our Approach

We believe online shopping should be simple. Product information should be understandable, prices should be clearly presented, and the path from discovering a product to placing an order should be as straightforward as possible.

PRASUN SHOP therefore emphasizes a clean interface, organized product categories, responsive browsing, and practical product information.

Technology & Engineering Perspective

PRASUN SHOP is founded by Prasun Barua, an engineer with professional experience in electrical engineering, renewable energy, solar PV systems, and technology-related projects.

This technical background influences the way we evaluate and present technology-related products. The objective is not simply to offer more products, but to make the catalog useful for customers looking for practical technology solutions.

Product Categories

Our store currently focuses on a broad range of technology categories, including solar and lighting products, batteries, chargers, power banks, cables, earphones, headphones, modems, routers, laptops, power tools, cameras, and smart home products.

Product availability and selection may change over time as suppliers, inventory, pricing, and product lines are updated.

Independent Store

PRASUN SHOP is an independent online store. Products may be sourced through third-party fulfillment and dropshipping partners, allowing customers to access a wider range of products without requiring traditional retail inventory at our location.

Because products can come from different suppliers and fulfillment locations, shipping availability, delivery times, and product specifications may vary by item and destination.

Our mission: provide a clean, reliable, and practical destination for discovering technology products that can genuinely be useful in everyday life and work.
