About Prasun Shop

Independent engineering publications built for clarity and impact.

Prasun Shop is an independent digital publishing platform founded and authored by Prasun Barua. Our mission is to bridge the gap between complex industrial theories and practical, everyday application.

From the fundamentals of residential solar systems and electrical engineering to the precise science of modern semiconductor manufacturing, every guide is written with one core focus: clarity. We strip away unnecessary academic jargon to deliver structured, highly actionable knowledge that helps professionals, students, and homeowners make confident technical decisions.

All books are written exclusively by Prasun Barua and provided as high-resolution digital PDFs, ready for instant download the moment your checkout is complete.
