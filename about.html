About PRASUN SHOP

Technical literature & practical guides designed for clarity and intuitive understanding.

PRASUN SHOP is an independent digital bookstore created and curated by engineer and technical author Prasun Barua. The store focuses on delivering structured, practical, and accessible educational guides across engineering disciplines, modern technology, and renewable energy concepts.

Author Background & Mission

Engineered topics can often feel unnecessarily complex. The objective of every eBook available in this store is to demystify technical subjects—whether covering solar PV testing, semiconductor manufacturing, or fundamental electrical engineering—through intuitive explanations and clear structure.

Digital Delivery & Security

To provide a smooth, safe, and instant purchasing experience, all transactions on PRASUN SHOP are processed through merchant partner Lemon Squeezy, ensuring encrypted checkout and immediate delivery of downloadable PDF files.
