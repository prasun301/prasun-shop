About PRASUN SHOP

A simple online store for useful technology, electronics, smart devices, renewable energy products, and everyday essentials.

PRASUN SHOP is an independent online store created by engineer and technical professional Prasun Barua. The store brings together practical products selected for technology-focused customers, including electronics, smart home devices, power and charging products, networking equipment, cameras, tools, and renewable-energy-related products.

Our Approach

We aim to keep online shopping straightforward: useful products, clear product information, simple navigation, and a clean purchasing experience. Products are selected with an emphasis on practicality, everyday usefulness, and relevance to modern technology.

Technology & Renewable Energy

With a professional background in electrical engineering and renewable energy, Prasun Barua has a particular interest in products related to electrical technology, electronics, solar energy, power systems, smart devices, and modern engineering applications.

Product Fulfillment

PRASUN SHOP works with third-party suppliers and fulfillment partners to make selected products available to customers internationally. Product availability, shipping options, delivery times, and applicable charges may vary by destination and product.
