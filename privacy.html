Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At PRASUN SHOP (accessible from our website), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by PRASUN SHOP and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit PRASUN SHOP, make a purchase, or attempt to contact us, we may collect personal information including but not limited to:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, shipping address, and telephone number.
  • Transactional Data: Details about products viewed, items added to your bag, and orders placed.
  • Log & Device Data: IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

PRASUN SHOP partners with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to PRASUN SHOP or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, PRASUN SHOP uses 'cookies' and browser localStorage to store information including visitors' preferences (such as shopping cart contents) and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize user experience and maintain site functionality.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various operational purposes, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and online store.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website content.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website.
  • Process product purchases and shopping cart sessions.
  • Communicate with you regarding customer support inquiries.
  • Detect and prevent fraud or security threats.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), consumers have rights including requesting disclosure of collected personal data and requesting that a business does not sell consumer personal data. PRASUN SHOP does not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@prasunbarua.com.
