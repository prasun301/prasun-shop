Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

PRASUN SHOP respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit shop.prasunbarua.com , how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy applies to information collected through the PRASUN SHOP website and related customer interactions.

1. Information We May Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the store, process orders, provide customer support, maintain website security, and improve the shopping experience.

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

  • Name, email address, telephone number, and shipping information submitted during an order or customer-support request.
  • Product, order, and transaction-related information necessary to fulfill purchases and provide support.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, referring pages, and approximate usage information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through contact forms, email, or other direct communications.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling customer orders.
  • Communicating with customers regarding orders, deliveries, questions, or support requests.
  • Maintaining and improving website functionality, performance, and usability.
  • Detecting, preventing, and investigating fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
  • Understanding general website usage and improving products, content, and customer experience.

3. Payment Processing

PRASUN SHOP may use third-party payment and commerce providers to process customer payments. Payment card information such as full card numbers, security codes, and other sensitive payment credentials are not intended to be stored directly on PRASUN SHOP servers.

When a third-party payment provider is used, payment information is submitted directly to that provider and is handled according to its own privacy policy, security practices, and terms.

Depending on the payment method available at checkout, the applicable payment provider may vary.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PRASUN SHOP and third-party service providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, maintain sessions, and improve the user experience.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Google Analytics

PRASUN SHOP uses Google Analytics to help understand how visitors interact with the website. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate geographic information, device information, browser information, referral sources, and interaction events.

Analytics information is used primarily for website measurement, performance analysis, and improvement.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

PRASUN SHOP may display advertising through Google AdSense and related Google advertising services.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising based on factors such as a user's interaction with this website and other websites.

Where applicable, users may manage advertising personalization through Google Ad Settings .

Additional information about Google's advertising practices is available through Google's relevant privacy and advertising resources.

7. Log Data and Security

Website infrastructure and hosting services may automatically record technical information in server logs. Such information can include IP addresses, request timestamps, browser information, requested URLs, response information, and security-related events.

These records may be used to maintain website reliability, investigate technical problems, protect the service, and detect malicious or unauthorized activity.

8. Third-Party Services

PRASUN SHOP may rely on third-party providers for services such as website hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, advertising, payment processing, order fulfillment, shipping, security, and other operational functions.

These providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is subject to their respective privacy policies and contractual obligations.

9. Product Fulfillment and Shipping

Where products are fulfilled through third-party suppliers or dropshipping partners, order information may need to be shared with the relevant supplier, fulfillment provider, shipping carrier, or logistics service so that the order can be processed and delivered.

Only information reasonably necessary for fulfillment and delivery should be shared for these purposes.

10. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including order administration, customer support, accounting, legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and security.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal or operational requirements.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Children's Privacy

PRASUN SHOP is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws applicable to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your data is processed.

Requests may be submitted through our Contact page .

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain privacy requests.

14. International Visitors

PRASUN SHOP may serve customers internationally. As a result, information may be processed by service providers located in countries different from your own.

Where applicable, such processing will be subject to appropriate contractual, technical, or legal safeguards required by relevant law.

15. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites and services. PRASUN SHOP is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Important: This Privacy Policy describes PRASUN SHOP's intended privacy practices in general terms. Specific practices may depend on the payment provider, fulfillment provider, analytics service, advertising platform, hosting infrastructure, and other services active on the website at a particular time.

17. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about how your information is handled, please contact PRASUN SHOP through our Contact page .
