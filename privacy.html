Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At PRASUN SHOP, accessible from prasunshop.com, protecting visitor privacy is one of our top priorities. This document outlines the types of information collected and how it is utilized.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase digital products on PRASUN SHOP, checkout process management and fulfillment are handled securely via our payment processor partner, Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record).

We may receive basic purchase details necessary for customer support and order fulfillment, including:

  • Your name and email address
  • Transaction timestamps and order details
  • Country location for tax compliance purposes

2. Payment Processing Security

We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial payment details directly on our servers. All financial transactions are processed securely by Lemon Squeezy using industry-standard encryption.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided during purchase is used strictly to:

  • Deliver digital product files and order receipts
  • Respond to customer support inquiries and download requests
  • Maintain necessary accounting and transaction records

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside third parties. Third-party partners (such as Lemon Squeezy) assist in operating our website and conducting transactions, operating under strict privacy standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or visit prasunbarua.com.
