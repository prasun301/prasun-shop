Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At PRASUN SHOP (accessible from our website), the privacy of our visitors is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by PRASUN SHOP and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our store or purchase an eBook, we may collect minimal personal information necessary to process your transaction or respond to support requests. This includes:

  • Contact details (such as your name and email address) submitted via contact forms or order checkouts.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and referring pages.

2. Third-Party Payment Processing (Lemon Squeezy)

All payments and eBook fulfillment are processed securely through our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. PRASUN SHOP does not store, collect, or process your full payment card details on our servers. Your payment transaction data is governed by Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

PRASUN SHOP uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to PRASUN SHOP or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Log Files

PRASUN SHOP follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

5. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data collected on this site. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please reach out through our Contact Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at prasunbarua.com or via our contact page.
