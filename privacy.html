Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

Prasun Shop ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or make a purchase from shop.prasunbarua.com.

2. Payment Processing & Data

Our store transactions are powered by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as our Merchant of Record. When you purchase an ebook, Lemon Squeezy collects your payment details, billing address, and email to process the transaction and deliver your digital files. We do not store or process your raw credit card information on our servers.

3. Information We Collect

When you visit the site, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, and time zone. Additionally, as you browse, we collect information about the individual web pages you view.

4. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, if you have questions, or if you would like to make a complaint, please contact us at contact@prasunbarua.com.
