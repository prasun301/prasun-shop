Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Because PRASUN SHOP offers non-tangible, irrevocable digital goods (PDF eBooks), instant access and downloads are provided immediately upon order confirmation.

1. General Refund Terms

All sales of digital eBooks are generally considered final. However, customer satisfaction is extremely important. We consider refund requests under the following conditions:

  • Duplicate Purchases: Accidental double order of the exact same eBook.
  • Technical File Corruptions: Unresolvable technical issues preventing file download or reading that cannot be resolved by support.

2. How to Request Assistance

If you experience an issue with your download link or made a duplicate purchase, please contact support within 14 days of purchase:

  • Visit our Contact Page
  • Include your order receipt email, order ID, and a brief description of the issue.

3. Processing Refund Requests

Approved refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy and returned to your original payment method. Funds typically post to your account within 5 to 10 business days depending on your payment provider.
