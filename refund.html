Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

At PRASUN SHOP, we want every customer to have a clear and fair purchasing experience. This policy explains how cancellations, returns, damaged products, incorrect items, missing deliveries, and refunds are handled for products purchased through shop.prasunbarua.com .

Because products may be fulfilled and shipped by third-party suppliers or logistics partners, certain return and refund conditions may depend on the product, destination, shipping method, supplier requirements, and applicable law.

1. Order Cancellation

You may contact us as soon as possible after placing an order to request cancellation.

A cancellation request cannot always be guaranteed because an order may already have been submitted to a supplier, processed, packed, or shipped.

When cancellation is successfully completed before fulfillment, the applicable product amount may be refunded to the original payment method, subject to any applicable payment-provider rules.

2. Returns

Returns may be accepted for eligible products when the product arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from the product ordered, subject to the conditions described below.

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, hygiene considerations, safety restrictions, customization, consumable status, supplier rules, or destination-country requirements.

A return must not be sent without contacting PRASUN SHOP first. Customers should wait for return instructions before sending any product back.

3. Damaged Products

If a product arrives damaged, please contact us promptly after delivery.

To help us investigate the issue, we may request:

  • Clear photographs of the damaged product.
  • Photographs of the outer shipping package and shipping label.
  • A description of the damage or problem.
  • Order information and any relevant delivery documentation.

Where a claim is approved, an appropriate solution may include a replacement, partial refund, full refund, or another reasonable remedy depending on the circumstances.

4. Incorrect or Missing Products

If you receive the wrong product, an incorrect quantity, or an item that appears to be missing from your shipment, contact us promptly so that we can investigate the order with the applicable fulfillment or shipping provider.

Please retain the packaging and shipping materials until the issue has been resolved, as these may be required as supporting evidence.

5. Defective Products

If a product is defective or does not function as reasonably expected when used according to its instructions, please contact us with details of the issue.

Depending on the product and supplier requirements, we may request photographs, video evidence, troubleshooting information, or other reasonable documentation before approving a replacement or refund.

6. Product Not Received

If tracking information indicates that a shipment is delayed, held by customs, or otherwise in transit, we may need to allow the applicable carrier investigation period to pass before determining the appropriate resolution.

If a shipment is confirmed as lost or otherwise not delivered after the applicable investigation, we may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate solution, subject to the circumstances and applicable carrier and supplier procedures.

7. Delivered but Reported Missing

If tracking indicates that a package has been delivered but you cannot locate it, please first check with household members, building management, neighbors, reception areas, or other safe delivery locations.

We may also work with the carrier to investigate the delivery. Resolution may depend on the carrier's investigation and available delivery evidence.

8. Change-of-Mind Returns

A change-of-mind return is not automatically guaranteed. Return eligibility may depend on the product, supplier requirements, destination, condition of the item, shipping costs, and applicable law.

Where a change-of-mind return is accepted, the customer may be responsible for return shipping or other costs unless otherwise required by applicable law or expressly agreed by PRASUN SHOP.

9. Product Condition for Returns

Where a return is authorized, the product should generally be returned in the condition required by the applicable return instructions, including any original packaging, accessories, manuals, labels, or other included items.

Products that have been intentionally damaged, materially altered, misused, improperly installed, or used contrary to manufacturer instructions may not qualify for a refund or replacement to the extent permitted by applicable law.

10. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • Approved cancellation before fulfillment.
  • Confirmed loss of the shipment.
  • Approved damaged, defective, or incorrect product claim.
  • Supplier-approved return where a refund is available.
  • Duplicate payment or other verified billing error.
  • Any circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

11. Refund Amount

The amount refunded will depend on the circumstances of the order and the applicable refund decision.

Where appropriate, the refund may include the amount paid for the affected product. Shipping charges, taxes, duties, customs fees, payment-provider fees, and other charges may be treated separately depending on the reason for the refund and applicable law.

12. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are generally issued to the original payment method used for the transaction unless another method is required or agreed.

After PRASUN SHOP initiates a refund, the time required for the funds to appear in your account depends on the payment provider, card issuer, bank, or other financial institution.

13. Shipping Costs

Shipping charges may be refundable in certain situations, such as where an incorrect product was shipped or a shipment was confirmed lost, depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

Shipping costs associated with voluntary returns or change-of-mind requests may be the customer's responsibility where permitted.

14. Customs, Duties, and Import Charges

Customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, and other charges imposed by the destination country are generally outside the direct control of PRASUN SHOP.

Such charges may not be refundable where they have already been paid to a government authority, carrier, customs broker, or other third party, unless applicable law or the relevant provider requires otherwise.

15. How to Request a Refund or Return

Please contact us through our Contact page as soon as reasonably possible after discovering an order issue.

Please provide:

  • Your order number.
  • The email address used during checkout.
  • The product name or affected item.
  • A clear description of the problem.
  • Photographs or other evidence where relevant.

Providing complete information helps us investigate the issue more efficiently.

16. Payment Provider Disputes

Customers should contact PRASUN SHOP first whenever possible so that we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve an order issue.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights or protections you may have under applicable payment-network rules, consumer-protection laws, or the terms of your payment provider.

17. Abuse and Fraud Prevention

Refund and replacement requests may be reviewed for fraud prevention and order verification.

We reserve the right to request reasonable evidence and to investigate repeated, inconsistent, or potentially fraudulent claims. Nothing in this section is intended to limit mandatory consumer rights.

18. Third-Party Fulfillment

Because PRASUN SHOP may use third-party suppliers and fulfillment providers, certain return addresses, replacement procedures, processing times, and eligibility requirements may vary between products.

Customers must follow the specific return or replacement instructions provided for their order.

19. Statutory Consumer Rights

This policy is intended to describe our general refund and return procedures. It does not remove, restrict, or replace any mandatory consumer rights or remedies available under the laws applicable to your purchase.

Important: Do not return a product before receiving instructions from PRASUN SHOP. An unauthorized return may be sent to an incorrect fulfillment location and may delay resolution of your claim.

20. Contact Us

For cancellation requests, damaged products, delivery problems, return requests, or refund questions, please visit our Contact page .
